About CreditPilot AI

Built for transparency and confidence.

CreditPilot AI LLC is developing technology designed to make credit report information easier to navigate. We believe people deserve clear explanations, practical tools, and control over their own information.

Meet the founders

Two Founders. One Mission. Your Financial Future.

CreditPilot AI was founded by Marc Ventolora and Jennifer Fahmy, two entrepreneurs who believe everyone deserves a fair opportunity to build a stronger financial future. Combining Marc's background in computer science and technology with Jennifer's focus on business management and operations, we're building CreditPilot AI to make credit information easier to understand and navigate. Through financial education and thoughtfully designed technology, our goal is to give people clearer information, practical tools, and greater confidence in their next steps. As equal partners, we share a commitment to transparency, integrity, innovation, and treating people with care.
