Privacy Policy — Development Draft

Effective: November 22, 2026 · Version: dev-2026-11-22

Draft for legal and technical review. Confirm hosting logs, retention, vendors, cookies, and rights-handling procedures before publishing to customers. This describes the intended development-preview workflow, not future live credit processing.

1. Information collected

For preview accounts, we collect the email address you provide, account authentication identifiers, email verification status, and account-related timestamps. Authentication is handled using Supabase. We may also receive technical information necessary to operate and secure the website, such as IP addresses and request logs through hosting and authentication providers.

2. How we use information

We use account information to register and authenticate users, secure the preview, manage access, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support inquiries. Do not upload real credit reports, Social Security numbers, government IDs, or financial documents to this development site.

3. Service providers

Our development infrastructure uses Netlify for hosting and serverless functions, Supabase for authentication and related database services, and Lob's test environment for owner-only fictional mail API testing. Fictional Lob tests do not require customer credit information. Provider processing is subject to their own terms and policies.

4. Sharing

We do not intend to sell preview account information. Information may be shared with service providers for operating the preview or where required by law. No live credit-bureau data integrations or physical mailings are enabled.

5. Security and retention

We use access controls and service-provider security features, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Account information may remain until deletion or as needed for security, legal, or operational purposes. Specific retention schedules are pending review.

6. Your choices

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your preview account information, subject to identity verification and applicable legal exceptions. Additional rights may apply based on your location.

7. Children

The preview is not intended for people under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will identify revised versions and dates. Material changes will be communicated where required.

9. Contact

Questions: support@mycreditpilotai.com.
